ESL Lesson Plans and Resources

  • Contrast: The multi-colored title is difficult to read, both because of the contract against the background and because the order of the colors seems to be chosen randomly. Using pure black for the text is also not very pleasing on the eyes.
  • Repetition: The only thing I see here that is consistent is the font used, but they could have used a less bolded font for the information about each activity.
  • Alignment: This is the most blaringly obvious issue with this website. Every bullet point gets progressively larger and larger, meaning they are no longer in alignment with each other. Additionally, the rainbow lines do not stretch with the size of the window, making them look incomplete.
  • Proximity: The use of a bullet list helps make clear distinction between different activities. However, this effect is nullified by the use of thick text for the descriptions of the activities and the increasing size of each bullet point.

Fazbear Archive

  • Contrast: The dark blue background with the orange, light blue, and white colors provides a very clear contrast. The dark blue also works very well to contrast the character designs themselves and put more emphasis on them. Additionally, the way the "case files" area changes from the brighter colors to a monochrome color scheme also provides good contrast, as it emphasizes the seriousness of the subject media compared to the rest.
  • Repetition: The consistent coloring, use of fonts, and set up up every information page is cohesive and consistent throughout the entire website.
  • Alignment: Everything is consistently aligned in the center of the page and along their respective containers. The pictures are also aligned well, utilizing the entirety of the space available to them without being too cluttered.
  • Proximity: Space is well-utilized, every section and image placed in neat grids next to one another. Nothing feels too cramped nor too spaced out, and every space is utilized to its potential.
